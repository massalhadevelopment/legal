طلّتي – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026 · العربية

This policy covers the app "طلّتي – جرّب قصة شعرك" (Talati). It explains what the app collects, why, who receives it, how long it is kept and how you can delete it. Talati lets you try a new look on your own photo with artificial intelligence (AI): you pick a style (hairstyle, hair colour, salon look, outfit, makeup, eyewear, age, slimmer or more muscular body, beard or nail art), add your photo, and the app shows you an AI-generated image of yourself with that look. It also offers a face-shape analysis and a seasonal colour analysis. Results are AI simulations for fun and inspiration; they may not match how a real haircut, colour or product will look on you.

1. Who we are

The data controller is MASSALHA FZCO (developer name Massalha Development), a company registered in the United Arab Emirates ("we", "us"). Contact for privacy questions and requests: support@massalhafzco.com.

2. Summary

  • No sign-up is needed. The app creates an anonymous account. Sign in with Apple is optional.
  • When you create a look, your photo is sent to our server, which sends it to our AI provider, OpenAI (USA), to generate the result. OpenAI does not use it to train its models.
  • Your uploaded photo is deleted from our server after 24 hours. Generated images are deleted from our server after 30 days, or earlier when you delete them in History or delete your account.
  • Your photos and results are never used to train AI, never used to recognise or identify anyone, and never used for advertising.
  • The app is for adults (18+) and for photos of yourself only.
  • No ads, no tracking, no advertising or analytics SDKs, and we never sell your data.

3. Your photos and the AI

  • Consent. Before your first photo, the app asks for your consent: the photo button says "موافق، التقط أو اختر صورة" and a line under it explains that your photo is sent to an external AI provider to create the result.
  • Which photos. A selfie or face photo for most styles, a photo of your hand for nail art, or a full-body photo for outfits and body edits. You take it with the camera or choose it from your photo library; the app only gets the photo you pick. Before sending, the app resizes the photo (long side up to 1024 pixels, JPEG); the resized copy carries no location data.
  • Only your own photo, adults only. Upload only photos of yourself. Don't upload photos of other people, and never photos of children. Our AI checks photos and refuses a photo that seems to show a child, more than one person, no face, or sensitive content.
  • Creating a look (try-on). Our server stores your photo in your private folder in Supabase Storage (servers in the EU) and sends it, with the style you picked, to OpenAI (USA), which generates the new image. Our server stores the generated image in the same private folder, and the app downloads it to your device. We also keep a record of each try-on (the kind of try-on, the style you picked, its status and time) so the result can be delivered, a retry is not charged twice, and usage limits work.
  • Face-shape and seasonal colour analysis. Your photo (and the gender you chose in the app, if any, so the advice fits you) is sent through our server to OpenAI, which returns a text analysis. Our server does not store the photo or the analysis; the result is kept on your device.
  • How long. Uploaded photos are deleted from our server 24 hours after upload. Generated images and try-on records are deleted after 30 days, or earlier when you delete a look in History or delete your account.
  • At OpenAI. OpenAI processes your photo only to give you the result, as our processor. It does not use data sent through its API to train its models, and may keep it for up to 30 days to monitor abuse, then deletes it. We send OpenAI a one-way coded (hashed) version of your account ID for abuse detection, never the ID itself, and never your name.
  • No face recognition. We don't create or store face templates or other biometric identifiers, and we never use your photos to identify you or anyone else.

4. Data that stays on your device

The following is stored on your device and is not sent to us:

  • History: copies of your generated looks, the photos you used for them and your analysis results.
  • Your settings, the gender you chose (so the app addresses you correctly and shows fitting styles), your consent choices and favourites.

Data on your device can be included in your own device backup (for example iCloud Backup) if you use one. If you tap Save, the app saves the image to your photo library (with your permission); if you tap Share, the image goes to the app you choose. Deleting a look in History, deleting your account in the app or deleting the app removes the app's copies from the device (images you saved to your photo library or shared stay there).

5. Your account and purchases

  • Anonymous account. On first launch the app creates an anonymous account with Supabase (servers in the EU). It holds a random user ID and the date it was created, so that purchases, usage limits and your try-ons work. It does not contain your name or email.
  • Sign in with Apple (optional) lets you keep your subscription on a new device. The app asks Apple only for your name, not your email. We receive your Apple account ID and, if you choose to share it, your name, which we keep with your account. We also keep an Apple sign-in token, encrypted, only so that we can revoke your Apple sign-in when you delete your account.
  • Purchases. Payments are handled by Apple; we never see your card details. RevenueCat (USA) receives your purchase receipts, the products you bought and your anonymous user ID (which is your RevenueCat ID) to manage your subscription, and tells our server whether you are a subscriber or in the free trial. RevenueCat also uses these records to give us subscription statistics (for example how many people start or cancel a subscription).
  • Usage counters. To apply the free trial's 5 looks and the fair-use limits, our server counts how many looks and analyses you used. To stop one free trial from being used again by deleting the account or reinstalling, we also keep a counter per purchase under a one-way coded (hashed) ID that does not contain your user ID; it is kept for up to 1 year, also after you delete your account.

6. Feedback and support

If you send feedback in the app (Settings → feedback), we store the type you chose (for example a suggestion, a problem or a refund question), your text, any screenshots you attach, your user ID, the app version and the device model and iOS version, and your email address if you add one, so we can look into it and answer you. Feedback and its screenshots are deleted after 180 days, or earlier when you delete your account. We can't issue refunds ourselves: refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. When you email us, we keep the conversation for as long as we need it to handle your request.

7. Notifications

The app may send local notifications created on your device (for example, to tell you that your look is ready). We have no push notification server and send no marketing notifications. You can turn notifications off in your device settings.

8. No ads, no tracking, no analytics

The app shows no ads, does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, does not ask for the iOS tracking permission and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs (no Meta/Facebook SDK, Google Analytics or similar). We don't sell your data and we don't share it with data brokers. The app may show Apple's own rating prompt. If this changes in a future version (for example, to measure our ads), we will update this policy before that version is released, and the app will ask for your permission where the law or Apple requires it. Your photos and results would never be shared for advertising.

9. Why we use your data (legal bases)

  • To provide the app and your subscription (account, purchases, restoring purchases, try-ons and analyses, storing your results, answering your feedback): performance of our contract with you.
  • Sending your photo to the AI provider: your consent, given on the photo button. You can stop at any time by not adding new photos; to remove what we hold, delete your looks in History or delete your account.
  • Usage limits, abuse prevention, security and the per-purchase trial counter: our legitimate interests in keeping the service safe and affordable. You can object by emailing us.

10. Who receives your data

  • OpenAI (USA), our AI processor: generates the try-on images and the face-shape and seasonal colour analyses, and checks photos for safety.
  • Supabase (servers in the EU): hosts your account, stores uploaded photos and generated images in your private folder, try-on records, usage counters, feedback and screenshots, and the encrypted Apple sign-in token.
  • Expo (EAS Hosting), which runs on Cloudflare's network: runs our server code; your photos and requests pass through it in transit. Expo also delivers app updates: when the app checks for an update, Expo receives your IP address, the device platform and app version, and a random installation ID that is not linked to your account.
  • RevenueCat (USA): manages subscriptions and gives us subscription statistics.
  • Apple: payments, Sign in with Apple and the rating prompt.

Our providers process data under their data processing terms. Our hosting providers keep standard request logs (such as IP address and time) for a limited period under their own retention rules; our server briefly uses your IP address to limit abuse and does not store it. We do not write your photos or feedback text to our logs. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

11. International transfers

We are based in the UAE. Your data is stored in the EU (Supabase) and processed in the USA and other countries where OpenAI, RevenueCat, Expo, Cloudflare and Apple operate. Where the law requires it (for example for users in the EU or UK), these transfers rely on the providers' Standard Contractual Clauses or other approved safeguards.

12. How long we keep data

  • Uploaded photos on our server: 24 hours.
  • Generated images and try-on records on our server: 30 days, or earlier when you delete the look in History or delete your account.
  • Photos sent for a face-shape or seasonal colour analysis: not stored on our server.
  • Data at OpenAI: up to 30 days.
  • Data on your device (History, settings): until you delete it, delete your account or delete the app.
  • Anonymous or Apple account (and your name, if you shared it): until you delete your account.
  • Usage counters: until their period ends (daily counters: a few days; the free-trial counter: up to 1 year). Per-purchase trial counters (hashed, without your user ID): up to 1 year, also after account deletion.
  • Feedback and screenshots: 180 days, or until you delete your account if that is sooner.
  • Encrypted Apple sign-in token: until you delete your account (it is then revoked and deleted).
  • Purchase records at RevenueCat: until you delete your account. Apple keeps its own transaction records.
  • Hosting request logs: a limited period set by each provider.

13. Your rights and how to delete your data

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU or UK), you have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect what was done before). You can also complain to your local data protection authority. How to exercise them:

  • Delete one look: in the app, «إطلالاتي» → History → tap the trash icon on a look. This deletes it from your device and its generated image from our server.
  • Delete everything: in the app, Settings → delete account. This permanently deletes your account, your try-on records, usage counters (except the hashed per-purchase trial counter), feedback, all photos, generated images and screenshots in your folder, and your RevenueCat customer record, and revokes your Sign in with Apple. It also wipes the app's data and scheduled notifications on your device. It does not cancel an App Store subscription: manage that in your Apple ID settings. Copies kept by OpenAI for up to 30 days are not affected.
  • Anything else (for example a copy of your data): email support@massalhafzco.com. Because the account is anonymous, we may need details from the app to find your data; in-app deletion is the fastest way. We reply within 30 days.

14. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your photos and generated images are kept in a private storage folder that only our server can access; the app receives a generated image through a short-lived link (valid for 1 hour). The Apple sign-in token is stored encrypted. No method is 100% secure, so we keep photos on our servers for as short a time as possible.

15. Age and children

Talati is for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. Photos of children must not be uploaded, and the app refuses photos that seem to show a child. If you believe a child has used the app or that a child's photo was sent to us, contact us and we will delete it.

16. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app or in the App Store listing before they apply.

  • صنع إطلالة (التجربة). يحفظ خادمنا صورتك في مجلدك الخاص في Supabase Storage (خوادم في أوروبا)، ويرسلها مع الستايل الذي اخترته إلى OpenAI (الولايات المتحدة) التي تنشئ الصورة الجديدة. يحفظ خادمنا الصورة الناتجة في المجلد الخاص نفسه، ثم يحمّلها التطبيق إلى جهازك. ونحتفظ أيضاً بسجل لكل تجربة (نوعها، والستايل الذي اخترته، وحالتها، والوقت) حتى تصلك النتيجة، ولا تُحسب إعادة المحاولة مرتين، وتعمل حدود الاستخدام.

9. لماذا نستخدم بياناتك (الأساس القانوني)

  • لتقديم التطبيق واشتراكك (الحساب، المشتريات، استعادتها، التجارب والتحليلات، حفظ نتائجك، الرد على ملاحظاتك): تنفيذ العقد بيننا.
  • إرسال صورتك إلى مزوّد الذكاء الاصطناعي: موافقتك التي تعطيها بزر الصورة. يمكنك التوقف في أي وقت بعدم إضافة صور جديدة، ولإزالة ما نحتفظ به احذف إطلالاتك من السجل أو احذف حسابك.
  • حدود الاستخدام، ومنع إساءة الاستخدام، والأمان، وعدّاد التجربة لكل عملية شراء: مصلحتنا المشروعة في الحفاظ على خدمة آمنة وبتكلفة معقولة. يمكنك الاعتراض بمراسلتنا.

10. من يستلم بياناتك

  • OpenAI (الولايات المتحدة)، معالج الذكاء الاصطناعي لدينا: تنشئ صور التجربة وتحليلات شكل الوجه والألوان الموسمية، وتفحص الصور للتأكد من سلامتها.
  • Supabase (خوادم في أوروبا): تستضيف حسابك، وتحفظ الصور المرفوعة والصور الناتجة في مجلدك الخاص، وسجلات التجارب، وعدّادات الاستخدام، والملاحظات وصور الشاشة، ورمز Apple المشفّر.
  • Expo (EAS Hosting) وتعمل على شبكة Cloudflare: تشغّل كود خادمنا، وتمر عبرها صورك وطلباتك أثناء إرسالها. وتوصل Expo أيضاً تحديثات التطبيق: عندما يبحث التطبيق عن تحديث، تستلم Expo عنوان IP ونوع الجهاز وإصدار التطبيق ومعرّف تثبيت عشوائياً غير مرتبط بحسابك.
  • RevenueCat (الولايات المتحدة): تدير الاشتراكات، وتعطينا إحصاءات عن الاشتراكات.
  • Apple: المدفوعات، وتسجيل الدخول بحساب Apple، ونافذة التقييم.

يعالج مزوّدونا البيانات وفق شروط معالجة البيانات الخاصة بهم. يحتفظ مزوّدو الاستضافة بسجلات طلبات عادية (مثل عنوان IP والوقت) لفترة محدودة وفق قواعدهم، ويستخدم خادمنا عنوان IP الخاص بك لحظياً للحد من إساءة الاستخدام ولا يحفظه. لا نكتب صورك أو نص ملاحظاتك في سجلاتنا. قد نكشف البيانات إذا طلب القانون ذلك.

11. نقل البيانات خارج بلدك

نحن في الإمارات. تُحفظ بياناتك في أوروبا (Supabase)، وتُعالج في الولايات المتحدة ودول أخرى تعمل فيها OpenAI وRevenueCat وExpo وCloudflare وApple. حيث يتطلّب القانون ذلك (مثلاً للمستخدمين في الاتحاد الأوروبي أو المملكة المتحدة)، يعتمد هذا النقل على البنود التعاقدية القياسية لدى المزوّدين أو ضمانات معتمدة أخرى.

12. مدة الاحتفاظ بالبيانات

  • الصور المرفوعة على خادمنا: 24 ساعة.
  • الصور الناتجة وسجلات التجارب على خادمنا: 30 يوماً، أو قبل ذلك عند حذف الإطلالة من السجل أو حذف حسابك.
  • الصور المرسلة لتحليل شكل الوجه أو الألوان الموسمية: لا تُحفظ على خادمنا.
  • البيانات لدى OpenAI: حتى 30 يوماً.
  • البيانات على جهازك (السجل والإعدادات): حتى تحذفها، أو تحذف حسابك، أو تحذف التطبيق.
  • الحساب المجهول أو حساب Apple (واسمك إن شاركته): حتى تحذف حسابك.
  • عدّادات الاستخدام: حتى تنتهي مدتها (العدّادات اليومية: أيام قليلة، وعدّاد التجربة المجانية: حتى سنة). عدّادات التجربة لكل عملية شراء (مشفّرة، بدون معرّفك): حتى سنة، حتى بعد حذف الحساب.
  • الملاحظات وصور الشاشة: 180 يوماً، أو حتى تحذف حسابك إن كان ذلك أقرب.
  • رمز Apple المشفّر: حتى تحذف حسابك (ثم يُلغى ويُحذف).
  • سجلات الشراء لدى RevenueCat: حتى تحذف حسابك. وتحتفظ Apple بسجلات معاملاتها الخاصة.
  • سجلات طلبات الاستضافة: فترة محدودة يحددها كل مزوّد.

13. حقوقك وكيف تحذف بياناتك

بحسب مكان إقامتك (مثلاً في الاتحاد الأوروبي أو المملكة المتحدة)، يحق لك الوصول إلى بياناتك وتصحيحها وحذفها والحصول على نسخة منها، وتقييد استخدامها أو الاعتراض عليه، وسحب موافقتك في أي وقت (دون أن يؤثر ذلك على ما تم قبل السحب). ويحق لك أيضاً تقديم شكوى إلى هيئة حماية البيانات في بلدك. كيف تمارس هذه الحقوق:

  • حذف إطلالة واحدة: داخل التطبيق، «إطلالاتي» ← السجل ← اضغط على أيقونة سلة المهملات على الإطلالة. يحذفها هذا من جهازك ويحذف صورتها الناتجة من خادمنا.
  • حذف كل شيء: داخل التطبيق، الإعدادات ← حذف الحساب. يحذف هذا نهائياً حسابك، وسجلات تجاربك، وعدّادات الاستخدام (ما عدا عدّاد التجربة المشفّر لكل عملية شراء)، والملاحظات، وكل الصور والصور الناتجة وصور الشاشة في مجلدك، وسجلك لدى RevenueCat، ويلغي ربط حسابك بـ Apple. ويمسح أيضاً بيانات التطبيق والإشعارات المجدولة من جهازك. لا يلغي اشتراك App Store، ويمكنك إدارته من إعدادات Apple ID. لا يشمل الحذف النسخ التي تحتفظ بها OpenAI حتى 30 يوماً.
  • أي طلب آخر (مثل نسخة من بياناتك): راسلنا على support@massalhafzco.com. لأن الحساب مجهول، قد نحتاج إلى معلومات من التطبيق لإيجاد بياناتك، والحذف من داخل التطبيق هو الأسرع. نرد خلال 30 يوماً.

14. الأمان

تُرسل البيانات عبر اتصالات مشفّرة (HTTPS). تُحفظ صورك والصور الناتجة في مجلد تخزين خاص لا يصل إليه إلا خادمنا، ويستلم التطبيق الصورة الناتجة عبر رابط مؤقت (صالح لساعة واحدة). رمز Apple محفوظ مشفّراً. لا توجد وسيلة آمنة 100%، لذلك نحتفظ بالصور على خوادمنا أقصر مدة ممكنة.

15. العمر والأطفال

طلّتي للبالغين بعمر 18 عاماً فأكثر. التطبيق ليس موجّهاً للأطفال، ولا نجمع عن قصد بيانات شخصية من أي شخص دون 18 عاماً. يُمنع رفع صور الأطفال، ويرفض التطبيق الصور التي يبدو أنها لطفل. إذا كنت تعتقد أن طفلاً استخدم التطبيق أو أن صورة طفل أُرسلت إلينا، تواصل معنا وسنحذفها.
